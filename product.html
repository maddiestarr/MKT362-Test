About This Project

The Starr Home Collection is a fictional residential new construction concept created to showcase my interest in marketing, professional sales, and real estate.

The concept focuses on creating attractive, comfortable, and functional homes for people who are ready to take the next step into homeownership.

The Vision

A Home Designed Around You ♡

My goal with this concept is to combine thoughtful marketing with a strong understanding of what today's homebuyers are looking for.

Marketing Strategy

Marketing would focus on creating an emotional connection between potential buyers and the idea of owning a new home. Here's my approach:

01

Social Media

Use Instagram, TikTok, and other visual platforms to showcase home designs, features, and available properties.

02

Personal Selling

Build relationships with prospective buyers through personalized communication and sales experiences.

03

Community Marketing

Highlight neighborhood amenities, local experiences, schools, and community features.

04

Open Houses

Create welcoming open-house experiences that allow buyers to visualize themselves living in the home.
