About Me

♡

Hi! My name is Madeline Starr, and I am a senior at Iowa State University. I am majoring in Marketing and am also pursuing my Professional Sales Certificate.

I have always enjoyed connecting with people, learning how businesses grow, and understanding what motivates people to make decisions. My experiences at Iowa State have allowed me to explore those interests while developing my communication, sales, leadership, and relationship-building skills.

Outside of the classroom, I am involved in several organizations on campus. I am a member of Delta Zeta Sorority, Sales Club, Marketing Club, and the Real Estate Development Club. These organizations have given me opportunities to meet new people, build professional relationships, and grow as a leader.
